# Swiggy Restaurant Data Analysis

## Project Overview

This project provides a comprehensive analysis and visualization of restaurant data from Swiggy using **Excel** for cleaning and **Power BI** for dashboard creation. The goal is to uncover key business insights to optimize delivery performance, pricing strategy, market expansion, and customer satisfaction.

---

## Project Goals & Objectives

### Goals
- Analyze Swiggy restaurant data to derive business-critical insights
- Visualize key performance indicators using Power BI

### Objectives
- Evaluate restaurant performance based on ratings, reviews, and cuisine
- Build interactive dashboards for tracking food trends and operational metrics
- Optimize delivery times and pricing strategy based on regional patterns
- Identify underserved markets and high-demand areas for expansion

---

## Dataset Overview
- **Variables include:**
  - `ID`, `Area`, `City`, `Restaurant`, `Price`, `Avg Ratings`, `Total Ratings`, `Food Type`, `Address`, `Delivery Time`

- Outlier analysis using scatter plots showed no significant issues

## Key Insights

- **Underserved Markets:**  
  Ashok Nagar, Bidhannagar, and Surat show potential for expansion  
  Ahmedabad emerging as a growth hub

- **Competitive Zones:**  
  In saturated areas like Rohini and Chembur, differentiation is key (speed, variety, uniqueness)

- **Cuisine Strategy:**  
  - Chinese is popular in multiple metros  
  - Cafés are in demand in Chennai, Bangalore, and Hyderabad  
  - QSRs (Quick Service Restaurants) thrive in Pune and Bangalore

- **Pricing & Ratings:**  
  - Most popular price range: ₹200–₹500  
  - Higher ratings often correlate with mid-range pricing  
  - Premium pricing requires value justification

- **Delivery Time Optimization:**  
  - Kolkata has the highest average delivery time (~68 min)  
  - Cloud kitchens recommended for zones like Rohini, Chembur, and Andheri East

---

## Business Recommendations

### Expansion Strategy
- Enter underrepresented cities with growing demand
- Prioritize high-traffic zones for visibility

### Cuisine-Based Planning
- Expand top-performing cuisines per city
- Launch niche formats like QSRs and cafés based on local preferences

### Pricing Strategy
- Standardize high-performing price ranges
- Use loyalty programs for retention

### Delivery Optimization
- Setup cloud and satellite kitchens
- Use live tracking, realistic ETAs, and delivery personnel scaling

### Visibility & Engagement
- Promote high-rated but lesser-known restaurants via:
  - Influencer partnerships
  - Referral discounts
  - Social media campaigns
